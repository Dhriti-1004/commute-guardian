# Commute and Travel Preferences

## Mode of Transport Rules
- If destination distance is under 1.5 km and precipitation probability is below 20%, walking is preferred.
- If destination distance is between 1.5 km and 8 km and there is no rain, riding a two-wheeler or bike is preferred.
- If precipitation probability is 30% or higher, or if it is currently raining, avoid two-wheelers and walking; choose a cab or auto.
- If apparent temperature exceeds 38 degrees Celsius, avoid walking and two-wheelers during midday; prefer an air-conditioned cab.

## Arrival Buffers and Event Priority
- For exams, job interviews, or academic deadlines, require a minimum safety buffer of 25 minutes before start time.
- For movies, social meetups, or dining, a safety buffer of 10 minutes before start time is sufficient.
- For casual errands, zero buffer is acceptable.

## Traffic and Delay Thresholds
- If traffic delay exceeds 15 minutes on the primary route, consider leaving earlier or switching transport modes.
